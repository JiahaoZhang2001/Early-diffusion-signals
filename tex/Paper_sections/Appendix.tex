\section{Appendix: Original Twitter15/16 Benchmark Results}

This appendix preserves the fuller Twitter15/16 benchmark results that motivated the earlier version of the thesis. In the revised main text, Twitter15/16 are summarized as a comparative benchmark rather than treated as the primary empirical setting. To avoid losing the original benchmark detail, I retain the core benchmark figures here.

\subsection{Primary Benchmark Curves}

Figure~\ref{fig:app_twitter_veracity} and Figure~\ref{fig:app_twitter_reach} report the original Twitter15/16 size-based primary curves. These figures correspond to the benchmark values summarized in the main text: veracity peaking around AUC $=0.657$ at $k=20$ and reach peaking around $R^2=0.719$ at $k=180$ under the interaction-full specification.

Read together, these two figures show why the Twitter benchmark mattered in the earlier version of the thesis. Even in the cleaner native rumor-tree setting, veracity was never the stronger task. The benchmark already suggested that early diffusion was more informative for eventual reach than for veracity, although the veracity side looked somewhat better there than it does in FibVID. That is why the revised main text now uses Twitter15/16 mainly as a benchmark rather than as the sole empirical base.

\subsection{Model Family and Signal Validation}

The benchmark pattern in model-family comparisons and permutation checks is also retained here. As in the main text, the benchmark suggests a moderate veracity signal and a stronger reach signal, with tree-based regressors contributing more clearly on the reach side.

These benchmark figures are useful because they show that the revised thesis interpretation is not simply an artifact of switching to FibVID. The model-family figure already points to a task split in the benchmark setting: richer non-linear models matter more for reach than for veracity. The permutation figure also makes clear that benchmark veracity signal exists, but it still sits beside a stronger and more stable reach result. In that sense, the benchmark appendix helps show continuity between the earlier Twitter-centered analysis and the final FibVID-centered version of the thesis.
